About Spurax

We make everyday life easier to live.

Spurax is a family of brands built around the things people reach for every day: what they wear, what they eat and what they shop for.

Why we exist

Good things should never be hard to find.

Too often, the things people need are scattered. Good sellers are hard to discover, honest prices are hard to trust and a meal or an outfit takes more effort than it should.

Spurax began with a simple idea: bring these everyday needs together under one trusted name, and hold every part of it to the same standard of care.

We started with a marketplace that gives sellers a place to build and customers a place to shop with confidence. From there the family grew, with a fashion house for self-expression, a food market for fresh produce and a delivery kitchen for cooked meals. Each brand is different, but all of them share the same promise: quality you can count on.

Mission & vision

Where we are going, and why.

Our mission

To make buying, wearing and eating well simple and trustworthy, by giving sellers, vendors and customers a fair place to meet.

Our vision

To grow from Nigeria into a globally respected family of brands that people turn to first, for the everyday and the special.
