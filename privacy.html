Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

This is the corporate website of Spurax, a subsidiary of LIT-MAK HOLDINGS. This policy covers this website only. Each Spurax brand has its own privacy policy on its own platform.

2. What we collect

This website does not have accounts and does not ask you to sign up. We only receive information you choose to send us, such as:

  • your name, email address and message when you email us or use the contact form; and
  • anything else you decide to share in that message.

The contact form opens your own email app, so your message is sent through your email provider.

3. How we use it

We use what you send us only to reply to you, to handle your enquiry and to improve how we serve people. We do not sell your information.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy and tell you what is collected.

5. Sharing your information

We do not share your information with others, except where needed to answer your enquiry (for example, passing it to the right Spurax brand team) or where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep messages only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for our normal records, then delete them.

7. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing info@spurax.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive details such as passwords or card numbers by email.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date shown above.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at info@spurax.com.
