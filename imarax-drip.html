Imarax Drip is the Spurax fashion brand, built for people who want to be seen, on the street and in the corporate world. Streetwear and casual, in all styles, for everyone.

The name comes from a passion for visibility and self-expression. Many people want to wear what says who they are but do not know where to find it, or feel the world will not accept it. Imarax Drip closes that gap, with a bold, luxury and youthful feel and top-notch quality.
